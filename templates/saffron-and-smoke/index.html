Our Story

One flame, ten years of obsession

Chef Mara Ellison built Saffron & Smoke around a single oak-fired hearth — no gas, no shortcuts. Every dish touches smoke, ember or ash before it touches the plate.

We source from fourteen farms within ninety miles, dry-age in-house, and bake our saffron sourdough at dawn. The result is fine dining that still feels like it came from a fire someone loves.
